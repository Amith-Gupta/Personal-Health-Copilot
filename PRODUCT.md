# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary (inferred from the Altrix Labs brief, delegated by the owner): an Indian family caregiver, often the adult child, who keeps the paper prescriptions, lab reports and discharge summaries for parents and themselves and has to make sense of them between doctor visits. Secondary: hackathon judges at Altrix Labs scoring AI accuracy, architecture, UX, healthcare safety and the demo.

## Product Purpose
Turn a photo or PDF of a medical document into structured, verified facts (medicines, doses, test values, diagnoses, dates), explain them in plain English, Hindi or Telugu, and file them into one timeline per family member. Success: a caregiver understands what a report says and what to ask the doctor, without the app pretending to diagnose.

## Positioning
Every extracted fact stays traceable to the original document (shown side by side) and to an ABDM-aligned FHIR R4 record, so the plain-language layer never floats free of the source.

## Operating Context
Paper documents photographed on a phone, printed lab PDFs, bilingual Hindi/English prescriptions, handwritten Rx. Used at home, often on mobile, sometimes read aloud to elderly parents. Demo runs on a laptop for judges.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 15 frontend, FastAPI backend, Gemini vision for OCR/extraction, Groq (Qwen) for text extraction fallback and the copilot, Groq Whisper for voice.
- ABHA linking is a mock; no real ABDM gateway calls.
- Never fabricate medical data. When extraction fails, say so.
- Not a diagnostic or treatment tool; no dose-change advice.

## Evidence on Hand
Sample family records are generated by running the real extraction pipeline on synthetic documents and are labelled as sample data. No real patient data, testimonials or clinical validation exist; do not claim any.

## Product Principles
1. Source first: every value can be checked against the original page.
2. Honest uncertainty: show what the AI could not read instead of guessing.
3. Plain language, three languages, same facts.
4. One timeline per person; never mix family members' records.
5. Explain and prepare questions for the doctor; never diagnose or prescribe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Older readers and low-literacy users: large readable type, high contrast, read-aloud, Hindi and Telugu at full parity, works on small phones.
